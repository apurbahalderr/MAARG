- every endpoint starts with /api

/missions
POST - admins create a new mission for supply transport

/missions/my-mission
GET - fetches the active mission based on the truck no of the driver

/missions/[id]
GET - get the mission data based on missionId

/incidents
POST - report an incident from the frontend

/truck/[truckNo]
POST - update the vehicle location based on truck number
GET - get the vehicle location based on truck number
